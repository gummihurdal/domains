The Story, So Far

  1. Thirty minutes at Tibits, Basel — just long enough to want more.
  2. Two hours in Baden — an art gallery, Blume — and time moving too fast.
  3. Fondation Beyeler, au Pavillon, a bench on Münsterplatz — the evening love was quietly born. Sainte-Victoire in every colour and light — Cézanne returning, always discovering something new.
  4. The cabrio and a picnic in Alsace — France for an afternoon.
  5. A Basel restaurant, three at the table — meeting the best friend.
  6. Oberwil — a walk, a bright moon, a bench — and a first kiss.
  7. The Merian Gardens — everything in bloom.
  8. Aquabasilea — water and laughter.
  9. Hotel Panorama — Lake Zurich, setting the sky on fire.
  10. Lugano — the steps of Morcote, a balcony over the lake — the tenth date felt like a beginning.
  11. The road trip — Salzburg, then Slovenia: an alpine lake at dusk, and a heart-shaped shadow drifting over Bled.
  12. Leathers on, a Triumph Daytona 660 — Grace's first motorbike ride, out on the Alsace country roads.
  13. Backgammon on our own board at games.azurenexus.com — where a lone checker keeps proving Grace's rule.
  14. Cooking in Grace's kitchen — sweet potato curry, a chocolate cake, and a cheesecake from a Swedish Arla recipe — Leon supervising from the floor.
  15. The Lägern ridge — Baden to Regensberg along the rocky spine — thirteen and a half kilometres up on the crest. Watch a presentation of the hike.
  16. The last evening in July — hand in hand with Grace at the edge of the hill, watching a long band of gold laid slow and solemn across the ridge, the town going quiet and blue underneath it.
  17. — and the rest is still being written.
